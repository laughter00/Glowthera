Know what you use

Ingredient guide

A simple introduction to common skincare ingredients. Suitability varies by person and formulation.

Hydration
Glycerin

A humectant that helps attract water to the outer layer of skin.

Skin barrier
Ceramides

Lipids that help support the skin barrier and reduce moisture loss.

Hydration
Hyaluronic acid

A humectant used to help skin hold water; apply as directed by the product.

Uneven tone
Niacinamide

A form of vitamin B3 used in products for barrier support and uneven-looking tone.

Sun protection
Broad-spectrum SPF

Helps protect skin from UVA and UVB exposure. Choose SPF 30 or higher.

Moisture seal
Petrolatum

An occlusive ingredient that helps reduce water loss from the skin.
